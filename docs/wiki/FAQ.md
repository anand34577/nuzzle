## Does anything leave my computer?

Not apart from the one-time model download and anything you explicitly ask it to fetch. See [Safety and privacy](Safety).

## Which model does it use?

LFM3-Omni 2B, quantised to Q4_K_M, from LiquidAI. It takes audio and images directly, which is how it hears tone and sees the screen.

## Does it need an internet connection?

Only for the model download and for tools that read the web.

## Can it run on Linux or macOS?

Not yet. The bundled engine and the installers are Windows only.

## Can I use a different model?

You can choose your own files in Setup. The app is built around LFM3-Omni's audio support, so other models will lose voice features.

## How do I stop it right now?

Press `Ctrl+Alt+Space`.

## Where are my settings?

`%APPDATA%\dev.companion.desktop\settings.json`.
